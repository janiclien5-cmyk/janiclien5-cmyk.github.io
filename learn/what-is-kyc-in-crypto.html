What is KYC in crypto? (why exchanges ask for ID)

Signing up for an exchange and it wants your ID? That's KYC. Here's what it is, why it happens, and how to do it safely.

In one line

The identity check exchanges run at signup

KYC ("know your customer") is when a regulated exchange verifies who you are — usually your name, date of birth, and a photo ID. It's the same kind of check a bank does.

Why exchanges do it

It's a legal requirement in most places, aimed at preventing fraud, money laundering, and underage use. A platform asking for KYC is generally a sign it's operating within the rules — not a red flag by itself.

What it usually involves

  • Your basic details (name, date of birth, address).
  • A photo of a government ID.
  • Sometimes a quick selfie to match the ID.

How to do it safely

  • Use an established, reputable exchange — and check your local eligibility first.
  • Make sure you're on the real site, not a phishing lookalike.
  • Be suspicious of any random site, app, or person asking for your ID outside a proper signup.
