What is interest? (simple money basics)

Interest quietly shapes almost every money decision — loans, cards, savings, investing. Get this one idea and a lot of finance suddenly makes sense.

In one line

The price of borrowing — or the reward for saving

Interest is the cost of borrowing money, or the reward for saving/lending it. Borrow, and you pay extra on top of what you owe. Save, and the bank pays you a little for holding your money. The rate decides how much.

It can work for you — or against you

  • For you: on savings and investments, interest adds to your money over time (especially with compounding).
  • Against you: on debt, interest adds to what you owe — and high-interest debt can snowball fast.

Why the rate matters so much

A small difference in rate is a big difference in money over time. That's why paying off high-interest debt is so powerful, and why the rate on a savings account is worth checking.

Curious about crypto?

Put interest to work for you first

Let interest work for you — clear high-interest debt and build savings — before anything volatile, because crypto isn't a safe, predictable place to earn interest, and any crypto 'yield' still carries the asset's full price risk — it can swing hard either way. Get those basics in place first; then, if you're curious, learn about crypto carefully, only with money you can afford to lose. Start with the basics, safely.
