How to spot a fake crypto exchange

Fake exchanges and apps are a favorite scam — they look real until your money disappears. Here are the warning signs and how to stay safe.

The core defense

Type the address yourself

Most fake-exchange scams start with a link to a lookalike site — a URL one character off from the real one. Typing the official address yourself, or using a saved bookmark, defuses a huge share of these.

Warning signs

  • Lookalike / misspelled URL or a brand-new domain.
  • Promises of high or sure returns — real exchanges don't promise profits.
  • Pressure to deposit fast or "limited time" offers.
  • No real track record — no history, reviews, or verifiable company info.
  • You were contacted out of the blue and steered there by a stranger.

How to protect yourself

  • Stick to well-known, established exchanges.
  • Check reviews and company details before depositing anything.
  • Never let anyone rush or pressure you. When in doubt, don't deposit.
