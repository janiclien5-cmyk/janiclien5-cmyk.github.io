What is an NFT? (an honest beginner explainer)

NFTs got huge, then mostly crashed. Here's what they actually are, minus the hype — so you can understand the headlines without getting burned.

In one line

A unique token that points to a specific item

An NFT (non-fungible token) is a one-of-a-kind token on a blockchain, usually linked to digital art or a collectible. "Non-fungible" just means each is unique — unlike a coin, where one is interchangeable with another.

What they're used for

  • Digital art & collectibles — the most common use.
  • Membership / access — some grant entry to communities or perks.
  • In-game & identity items — an emerging, experimental area.

The honest part

A huge share of NFTs have lost most of their value, they can be hard to sell (illiquid), and the space is full of hype and scams. Owning the token also doesn't always mean you own the copyright to the art. None of that means NFTs are "bad" — it means caution beats FOMO.

The beginner takeaway

Understand NFTs before you ever consider one, never use money you can't afford to lose, and be extra alert to scams in this corner of crypto.
