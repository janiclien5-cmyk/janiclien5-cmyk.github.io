How to build an emergency fund

An emergency fund is the difference between a surprise being an inconvenience and being a crisis. Here's how to build one, even on a tight budget.

Why it matters

It's your financial shock absorber

A car repair, a medical bill, a lost job — a cushion means these don't push you into debt. It also means you never have to sell investments (or crypto) at a bad moment just to cover a bill.

How much to aim for

A common goal is 3–6 months of essential expenses. But don't let the big number stop you — any cushion beats none. Start with a small first target and build up.

Where to keep it

Somewhere safe and easy to access, like a separate savings account — not in anything volatile. The point is that it's there, in full, exactly when you need it.

How to start on a tight budget

  • Automate a small amount each payday — even a little adds up.
  • Funnel "found money" (refunds, bonuses) straight in.
  • Give it a name and a target so it feels real.
