What is a rug pull? (and how to avoid one)

A rug pull is one of crypto's most notorious scams — and beginners are the usual target. Here's how it works and how to spot one coming.

In one line

They hype it, take the money, and vanish

A rug pull is when the people behind a project pump it up, collect investors' money, then abandon it — "pulling the rug out" — leaving the token worthless. It's most common with brand-new, anonymous projects.

The warning signs

  • Anonymous team with no track record.
  • Huge hype, no real product — all promises, no substance.
  • Promises of massive returns or "sure" gains.
  • Pressure to buy fast before you can think.

How to protect yourself

Research the project: is the team public? Is the code audited? Does it solve a real problem? Be extra cautious with tiny new tokens, and never put in more than you can afford to lose. See also how to avoid crypto scams.
