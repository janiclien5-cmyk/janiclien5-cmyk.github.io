Market order vs limit order (for beginners)

When you buy or sell crypto, you choose how the order fills. The two basics — market and limit — trade off speed against price control. Here's the simple version.

The core trade-off

Speed vs price control

A market order fills immediately at the best available price — fast, but you don't control the exact price. A limit order fills only at a price you set (or better) — you control the price, but it might not fill at all.

Market order

Use it when you just want the trade done now. The risk: in a fast or thin market, the price you get can differ from what you saw (that gap is called slippage).

Limit order

Use it when the price matters more than speed. You set your price; the order waits until the market reaches it. The trade-off: if the market never hits your price, it simply doesn't execute.

Which should a beginner use?

Market orders are simplest for getting filled; limit orders help you avoid overpaying, especially when prices are jumpy. Many beginners prefer limit orders to stay in control — and always mind the fees and the spread.
