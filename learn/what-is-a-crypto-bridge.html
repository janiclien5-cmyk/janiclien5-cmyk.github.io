What is a crypto bridge? (moving between blockchains)

Different blockchains don't naturally talk to each other. A bridge is how assets move between them — useful, but with risks worth knowing before you use one.

In one line

A connector between blockchains

A crypto bridge lets you move assets from one blockchain to another — for example taking a token from one network to use it on a different one. It links networks that otherwise can't interact.

Why people use one

To access cheaper or faster networks (like a layer 2), to use an app that only exists on another chain, or to move into a different ecosystem. It's a core part of how the wider crypto world connects.

The real risk

Bridges have been a frequent target of major hacks because they hold a lot of locked funds in one place. For a beginner, they're an advanced tool — if you ever use one, stick to well-known bridges, move small amounts, and understand that bridging carries its own risk on top of normal crypto volatility.
