How to start crypto safely (a beginner's step-by-step)

No hype, no promises — just the calm, sensible order to do things in so you don't make the mistakes most beginners make.

Before anything else

Only use money you can afford to lose

Crypto is volatile, scams are common, and on-chain mistakes usually can't be reversed. None of that means "avoid it" — it means go slow, learn first, and never risk money you need.

Step 1 — Learn the basics first

The single biggest risk reducer is understanding what you're doing before you act: a wallet holds your own keys (no password reset), an exchange is where you buy and sell, and prices swing hard. A little learning prevents most beginner losses.

Step 2 — Pick a well-known exchange

Most beginners start on a reputable exchange to learn how buying, selling, and securing crypto actually works. Availability depends on your country, so check your local eligibility first.

Step 3 — Lock down your security

  • Turn on app-based 2FA (not SMS).
  • Use a unique, strong password for the account.
  • Never share your seed phrase — no real support team asks for it.
  • Verify every link and address before you click or send.

Step 4 — Start small

There's no prize for rushing. Begin with a small amount while you learn, and let understanding — not hype — guide what you do next.
