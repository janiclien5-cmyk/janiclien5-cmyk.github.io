How to avoid high crypto fees (beginner tips)

Fees are easy to ignore — and they quietly eat your money. Here are the kinds you'll meet and simple ways to pay less.

Why it matters

Small fees add up fast

A few percent here and there, trade after trade, compounds into real money. Knowing where fees hide is half the battle.

The fees you'll meet

  • Trading fees: a cut of each buy or sell (often "maker/taker" rates).
  • Network / gas fees: paid to the blockchain to process a transaction; they spike when it's busy.
  • Deposit / withdrawal fees: charged to move funds in or out.
  • The spread: the gap between the buy and sell price — an indirect cost.

Simple ways to pay less

  • Compare fee schedules before picking an exchange.
  • Avoid lots of tiny trades — each one is taxed by fees.
  • Transact when the network is quieter (gas fees fall).
  • Check the withdrawal network fee before you send — they vary a lot.
