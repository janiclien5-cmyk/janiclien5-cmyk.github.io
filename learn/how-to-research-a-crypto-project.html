How to research a crypto project (DYOR checklist)

"DYOR" — do your own research — is the habit that separates informed beginners from hype victims. Here's what to actually look at.

Why it matters

Most projects won't last

Thousands of coins exist and the large majority fade or fail. Researching first is how you avoid the obvious traps — and there are a lot of them.

The checklist

  • Use case: does it solve a real problem, or is it just hype and promises?
  • Team: are they public and credible, or anonymous with a big story?
  • Tokenomics: how many coins exist, and who holds most of them?
  • Activity: real development and a genuine community, or just paid hype?
  • Docs: does the whitepaper explain things clearly, or drown you in jargon?

Instant red flags

  • Promises of sure or easy returns.
  • Anonymous team with no track record.
  • Pressure to buy fast "before it's too late."
  • Hype with no working product behind it.

When in doubt, slow down. Missing one opportunity is far cheaper than falling for one scam.
