How to save for a house (a beginner's plan)

A house deposit feels huge — until you break it into a target, a timeline, and an automatic habit. Here's a calm, doable plan.

Step 1

Set a clear target and timeline

Decide roughly how much deposit you're aiming for and by when. Divide it into a monthly number — a big goal becomes a series of small, trackable steps.

Step 2 — Automate a dedicated pot

Open a separate savings account just for the house, and set up an automatic transfer each payday. Keeping it separate stops it quietly getting spent.

Step 3 — Free up more by cutting big costs

Target your largest expenses — rent, transport, subscriptions — rather than tiny treats. A simple budget and tracking your spending show where the room is.

Step 4 — Keep it safe

Money you'll need within a few years belongs somewhere safe and accessible — like a high-yield savings account — not in volatile assets that could drop right when you need them.

Curious about crypto?

Not your house-deposit money

A house deposit is exactly the kind of money that should not go into crypto — it's too volatile for a near-term goal. Keep that safe. If you're curious to learn about crypto separately, with money you can afford to lose, start with the basics, safely.
