How to save money (habits that actually stick)

Saving isn't about willpower or giving up everything. It's about a few simple habits that make saving the default. Here they are.

The one that matters most

Pay yourself first

Automate a transfer to savings the day you're paid — before you can spend it. Saving what's "left over" almost never works; saving first does.

Automate everything you can

The less you have to think about it, the more it happens. Automatic transfers turn good intentions into results without daily willpower.

Cut the quiet leaks

  • Unused subscriptions you forgot you had.
  • Account and card fees you can avoid.
  • Small daily habits that quietly add up over a month.

Give it a goal

"Save money" is vague; "save $1,000 for an emergency fund" is motivating. Name the goal and track it — progress is the best fuel.
