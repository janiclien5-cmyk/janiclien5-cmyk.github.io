Public vs private keys (explained simply)

Two keys power every crypto wallet. Mix them up and you risk everything — but the difference is easy once you picture a mailbox.

The mailbox analogy

One to receive, one to spend

Your public key (and the address from it) is like your mailbox — you can share it so people send you crypto. Your private key is the only key that opens it and lets you spend — so it must stay secret.

Public key / address

Safe to share. It's how others send you crypto and how transactions are checked. Sharing it can't drain your wallet.

Private key

Total control. Whoever has it can move your funds. It's backed up by your seed phrase, which is why that phrase matters so much. Never share it, type it into a site, or store it online.

The takeaway

Share the public side freely; guard the private side with everything. Anyone asking for your private key or seed phrase is trying to rob you.
