How to withdraw crypto safely

Moving crypto off an exchange is routine — but one wrong detail can lose it for good. Here's the short checklist that keeps withdrawals safe.

Why it matters

Withdrawals can't be reversed

Once it's sent, it's sent. The wrong address or wrong network usually means it's gone — so a minute of checking is the cheapest insurance there is.

The checklist

  • Secure first: confirm 2FA is on and you're on the real site, not a phishing copy.
  • Address: copy-paste it (never type), and verify the first and last characters.
  • Network: the sending and receiving network must match exactly.
  • Memo/tag: some coins require one — missing it can lose the funds.
  • Fees & minimums: check the withdrawal fee and any minimum amount.
  • Test first: send a tiny amount, confirm it arrives, then send the rest.

This is the same care you'd use sending crypto anywhere — because a withdrawal is just a send from the exchange.
