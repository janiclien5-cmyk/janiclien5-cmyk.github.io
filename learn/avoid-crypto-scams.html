How to spot & avoid crypto scams

Almost every crypto scam runs on the same two ingredients: a promise of easy money and pressure to act fast. Learn the patterns and they stop working on you.

The one rule

If it promises easy or "sure" profits — walk away

No legitimate person promises sure profits or doubles your money. The moment something sounds too good, or someone rushes you, treat it as a scam until proven otherwise.

The common scams

  • Fake giveaways. "Send 1 coin, get 2 back." You never get anything back.
  • Romance / investment scams. A warm stranger who eventually steers you to a "sure" platform.
  • Lookalike & phishing sites. A URL one character off from the real one, built to steal your login.
  • "Support" impostors. DMs from fake staff asking for your seed phrase or password.
  • Pump & dump hype. Coordinated hype to inflate a coin so insiders can sell on you.

The rules that keep you safe

  • Never share your seed phrase — no real support team ever asks.
  • Verify every link and address; type official URLs yourself.
  • Turn on app-based 2FA and use a unique password.
  • Slow down. Urgency is the scammer's main tool.

If you've already been scammed

Act fast: stop further transfers, secure accounts with new passwords and 2FA, revoke risky wallet token approvals, report it to the platform and authorities, and warn others. Recovery is rare, so fast containment matters most.
