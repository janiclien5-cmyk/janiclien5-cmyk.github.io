What is passive income? (an honest look)

"Passive income" is one of the most hyped — and most misunderstood — money phrases. Here's the honest version, minus the dream-selling.

The honest definition

Income with little ongoing effort — after a lot of upfront work

Passive income is money that keeps coming with little day-to-day effort. But the word "passive" hides the truth: it almost always takes significant upfront work, money, or both to set up. It's rarely effortless from day one.

More realistic examples

  • Interest from savings (needs capital saved first).
  • Returns from long-term investments (needs money invested, and carries risk).
  • Royalties from something you created (needs the upfront creation).
  • Renting out something you own (needs the asset and upkeep).

The scam warning

"Easy, sure-thing passive income" pitches are a classic scam — especially in crypto, with fake "yield" platforms and doublers. If someone promises sure, effortless returns, that's a red flag, not an opportunity. See how to spot scams.
