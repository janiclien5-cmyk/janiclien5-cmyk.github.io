Money tips for young adults

Starting young is the biggest money advantage there is. A few simple habits in your 20s set up decades of easier choices later.

Your superpower

Time is on your side

The earlier you start, the more compounding does the heavy lifting. Even small amounts saved young can outgrow much larger amounts saved later. That head start is yours to use.

The habits worth starting now

  • Spend less than you earn — and avoid lifestyle creep as income grows.
  • Build a small emergency fund so surprises don't become debt.
  • Build credit responsibly — pay cards in full, on time.
  • Dodge high-interest debt; pay off any you have fast.
  • Keep it simple — boring, consistent habits beat clever tricks.

Don't fall for the hype

Young people are the biggest target for "fast money" pitches. The unsexy basics above genuinely build wealth; the shortcuts usually don't.

Curious about crypto?

Foundations first, hype never

Crypto can be exciting, but it's volatile and full of scams aimed at beginners. Build your foundation first, and if you're curious, learn carefully with only money you can afford to lose. Start with the basics, safely.
