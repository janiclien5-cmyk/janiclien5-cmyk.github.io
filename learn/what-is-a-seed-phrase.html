What is a seed phrase? (and how to protect it)

If you learn one crypto-security thing, make it this. Your seed phrase is the single most important — and most targeted — secret you'll have.

In one line

The 12–24 words that ARE your crypto

A seed phrase (or recovery phrase) is a list of 12 to 24 words that can restore your wallet anywhere. Whoever has it controls the crypto — so it's not just a password, it effectively is your crypto.

The rules — no exceptions

  • Write it on paper and store it offline, somewhere safe.
  • Never type it into a website or app to "verify", "claim", or "connect".
  • Never photograph it or save it in the cloud, email, or notes app.
  • Never share it — no real support team, giveaway, or admin will ever ask.

Why scammers chase it

Because it's instant, total access. Most "your wallet has an issue, verify your phrase" messages and fake support DMs exist purely to get it. Anyone who asks for your seed phrase is trying to rob you — full stop. See how to spot scams.
