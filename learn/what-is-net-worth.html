What is net worth? (and how to calculate yours)

Income tells you what flows in; net worth tells you where you actually stand. It's the real money scoreboard — and it's simple to work out.

In one line

What you own minus what you owe

Net worth = your assets (what you own) minus your liabilities (what you owe). One number that captures your overall financial position — far more telling than income alone.

How to calculate it

  • Add up assets: cash, savings, investments, property value, etc.
  • Subtract liabilities: loans, credit-card balances, other debts.
  • The result — positive or negative — is your net worth. A negative number just means a starting point.

Why track it

Because it captures the whole picture: paying down debt and growing savings both move it up, even in a month you didn't earn more. Checking it a few times a year shows whether you're truly moving forward.

Curious about crypto?

A small, volatile slice at most

If crypto is ever part of your net worth, keep it a small, high-risk slice — and remember it can swing sharply. Foundations first; learn crypto carefully and only with money you can afford to lose. Start with the basics, safely.
