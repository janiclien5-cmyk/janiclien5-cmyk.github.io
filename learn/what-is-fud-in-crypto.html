What is FUD in crypto? (and how to handle it)

If FOMO is the greed side of crypto emotion, FUD is the fear side. Both cloud judgment — here's how to think clearly through it.

In one line

Fear, uncertainty and doubt

FUD is negative sentiment or scary claims that spread fast in crypto. Sometimes it's deliberate manipulation to push a price down; sometimes it's a genuine red flag worth heeding. The skill is telling the two apart.

Manipulation or real warning?

  • Possibly manipulation: vague scary claims, no sources, perfectly timed to a dip.
  • Possibly real: specific, verifiable concerns about a project's tech, team, or finances.

How to handle it

Don't react emotionally to a headline. Check the source, look for facts, and tie it back to your own research and plan. A calm, slow decision beats a panicked one — just as it does with FOMO.
