How to buy bitcoin for beginners (step by step)

The actual process is simpler than it looks. Here's how it works, plus the basics that keep you from overpaying or getting caught out — no hype.

Before you buy

Only use money you can afford to lose

Bitcoin's price swings hard. Buying a small amount to learn is very different from betting money you need. Start small, learn how it works, and go from there.

Step 1 — Choose an exchange (and check eligibility)

Most beginners buy through a reputable exchange. Availability depends on your country, so check your local eligibility first. See how to choose a crypto exchange for what matters.

Step 2 — Create and secure your account

  • Sign up and complete any required ID verification (KYC) — normal for regulated platforms.
  • Turn on app-based 2FA and use a unique, strong password.
  • Bookmark the real site so you never log in through a fake link.

Step 3 — Add funds and buy a small amount

Deposit an amount you're comfortable learning with, then place a small order. A market order buys at the current price; a limit order only fills at a price you set. Mind the fees.

Step 4 — Understand where it's stored

Crypto you buy usually sits in your exchange account at first. As you learn, understand the difference between that and a self-custody wallet you control. Never share your seed phrase.
