What is Web3? (a plain-English explainer)

"Web3" gets used a lot and explained rarely. Here's the simple idea behind it — and an honest take on the hype.

In one line

The idea of a more user-owned internet

Web3 is the vision of an internet built on blockchains where you own your accounts, data, and digital items — instead of big platforms owning them. It's a direction and a work in progress, not a finished thing.

A quick history

  • Web1: the early read-only internet — you mostly just viewed pages.
  • Web2: today's interactive web — you create content, but platforms own it.
  • Web3: the proposed next step — you own your identity and assets via blockchains.

What's part of it

Crypto tokens, NFTs, DeFi, and DAOs (community-run organizations) all sit under the Web3 umbrella, mostly powered by smart contracts.

The honest part

Web3 is genuinely interesting but also full of hype, half-built products, and scams riding the buzzword. Treat grand promises with healthy skepticism, and judge each project on what it actually does.
