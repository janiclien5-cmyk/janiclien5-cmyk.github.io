What is a crypto whitepaper? (and how to read one)

A whitepaper is where a crypto project explains itself — and reading it is one of the simplest ways to separate substance from hype.

In one line

The project's own explanation

A whitepaper is a document a project publishes to explain what it does, how it works, and why it exists. It's the pitch plus the technical overview — and reading it is part of doing your own research.

What a good one explains

  • The problem it actually solves.
  • How the tech works — clearly, not buried in buzzwords.
  • The token's purpose and supply — and who holds it.
  • The team and a realistic plan.

Red flags when you read one

  • Big promises with no clear technology behind them.
  • Vague buzzwords instead of plain explanations.
  • Anonymous team or copied content.
  • Heavy focus on price going up rather than the actual product.
