Crypto terms for beginners (a plain-English glossary)

The words you'll keep running into, explained in one line each — no jargon, no hype. Bookmark it for when something doesn't make sense.

The basics

  • Wallet — an app or device that holds your own keys. No password reset, so back it up.
  • Exchange — where you buy, sell, and trade crypto. Secure it with a strong password + 2FA.
  • Blockchain — a shared, public record of transactions no single party can quietly rewrite.
  • Coin vs token — a coin runs on its own blockchain; a token is built on top of another one.
  • Private key / seed phrase — the secret that controls your crypto. Never share it with anyone.

Security & safety

  • 2FA — a second login step (use an app, not SMS) that blocks most account takeovers.
  • Cold vs hot wallet — cold is offline (safer for holding); hot is online (handier for small amounts).
  • Phishing — fake sites/messages built to steal your login or seed phrase. Verify every link.
  • Rug pull — a project that takes the money and disappears. Be wary of hype with no substance.

Money & trading

  • Stablecoin — crypto designed to track a stable value (e.g. the dollar). The peg is a claim, not a promise.
  • Market cap — price x circulating supply. A size measure, not how "cheap" a coin is.
  • Gas fee — the network fee for a transaction; it rises when the network is busy.
  • Volatility — how sharply a price swings. Crypto's is high, in both directions.
  • DCA — dollar-cost averaging: investing a fixed amount on a schedule instead of all at once.
  • DYOR — "do your own research" before acting on anything you see online.
