What is a stop-loss? (crypto risk basics)

A stop-loss is one of the simplest tools for managing risk — it decides your exit before emotions can. Here's how it works and what it can't do.

In one line

An automatic "sell if it drops to here"

A stop-loss is an order that automatically sells if the price falls to a level you set, capping how much you could lose on a position. It takes the decision out of the heat of the moment.

Why people use it

  • Caps the downside on a position to a level you chose in advance.
  • Removes emotion — no freezing or "hoping it comes back".
  • Set and forget — you don't have to watch the screen all day.

The honest limits

A stop-loss reduces risk but doesn't promise an exact outcome. In fast or thin markets, the actual sale can happen at a worse price than your stop level (slippage). And set too tight, normal volatility can trigger it early. It's a tool, not a safety net.

A stop-loss is something you set on the exchange when you place a trade — worth knowing how to find before you ever need it.
