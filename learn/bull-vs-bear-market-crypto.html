Bull vs bear market: what they mean

Two words you'll see constantly in crypto. Here's what they mean, how they feel, and why chasing the timing usually backfires.

The quick version

Up and optimistic vs down and fearful

A bull market is a sustained stretch of rising prices and optimism. A bear market is a sustained stretch of falling prices and pessimism. (A bull thrusts up; a bear swipes down — that's the memory trick.)

How each one feels

  • Bull: excitement, FOMO, "everyone's getting rich" stories — the riskiest time to chase hype.
  • Bear: fear, boredom, "it's over" headlines — when many give up right before they'd have learned the most.

Why timing is so hard

You only know a top or bottom after it happens. Even professionals rarely call them consistently. Trying to perfectly time the market is how a lot of beginners buy high and sell low.

The level-headed approach

Instead of predicting cycles, focus on what you control: understand what you own, manage risk, avoid hype and panic, and only use money you can afford to lose. A calm plan beats a hot take in both markets.
