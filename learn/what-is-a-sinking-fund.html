What is a sinking fund?

It's the quietly brilliant trick that turns scary one-off bills into non-events. Once you use sinking funds, surprise expenses stop wrecking your month.

In one line

Save a little now for a bill you know is coming

A sinking fund is money you set aside gradually for a known, planned expense — a holiday, car repairs, annual insurance, the holidays — so when the bill arrives, the money is already there instead of going on a credit card.

Sinking fund vs emergency fund

  • Emergency fund: for unexpected surprises (a job loss, a sudden bill).
  • Sinking fund: for expenses you know are coming but aren't monthly.

You plan a sinking fund on purpose, ahead of time — that's the whole magic.

How to set one up

  • Pick the goal and its rough cost (e.g. $600 for holidays).
  • Divide by the months you have (e.g. $50/month for a year).
  • Automate that small transfer to a labeled savings pot.
