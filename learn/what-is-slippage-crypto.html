What is slippage in crypto?

Ever clicked buy at one price and ended up paying another? That's slippage. Here's why it happens and how to keep it small.

In one line

The gap between expected and actual price

Slippage is the difference between the price you expected and the price your trade actually filled at. Because prices move and orders fill against whatever supply is available, the final price can differ — a little, or a lot.

What causes it

  • Volatility: fast-moving prices change between click and fill.
  • Low liquidity: thin markets can't absorb your order at one price.
  • Big orders: large trades eat through several price levels.

How to reduce it

  • Use limit orders to set your price.
  • Stick to more liquid coins and larger markets.
  • Avoid huge orders in thin markets; break them up.
  • Many platforms let you set a slippage tolerance — use it.
