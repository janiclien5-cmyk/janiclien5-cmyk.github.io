What is a money market account? (beginner basics)

It sounds technical, but a money market account is really just a savings account with a few extra features. Here's the plain version.

In one line

A savings account with easier access

A money market account is a savings account that often pays competitive interest while giving easier access to your money — sometimes with limited checks or a card. It blends saving and spending features, while keeping your money safe.

How it differs from a savings account

  • Access: may allow limited checks or a debit card.
  • Rate: can be competitive, sometimes higher than a basic account.
  • Minimums: often needs a higher minimum balance to avoid fees.

What to check

Compare the interest rate, fees, minimum balance, and access rules against a high-yield savings account. The best choice depends on how much you'll keep in it and how often you need to touch it.
