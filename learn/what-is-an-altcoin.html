What is an altcoin? (a beginner's explainer)

You'll hear "altcoin season" and "the next big altcoin" constantly. Here's what the word actually means — and why caution matters more here.

In one line

Any crypto that isn't Bitcoin

"Altcoin" is short for "alternative coin" — basically any cryptocurrency other than Bitcoin. There are thousands, from major established projects to tiny, speculative tokens.

The main kinds you'll meet

  • Large established projects like Ethereum and other top coins.
  • Stablecoins designed to hold a steady value.
  • Utility tokens tied to a specific app or platform.
  • Memecoins — joke/hype-driven and extremely risky.

Why altcoins are riskier

Most altcoins are smaller, newer, and more volatile than Bitcoin, and the large majority fade or fail over time. Some are genuine projects; many are hype or scams. That's why researching a project matters even more here, and why market cap tells you more than price.

Learn the basics before chasing alts

Beginners are usually better off understanding the fundamentals and security first, not chasing the "next big altcoin." Start with the basics, safely, and only risk money you can afford to lose.
