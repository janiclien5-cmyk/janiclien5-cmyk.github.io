How to cash out crypto (convert it back to money)

No hype, no promises — just the honest answer to a fair question every beginner has: can I get my money back out, and how?

The short answer

Yes — you sell it, then withdraw the cash

Cashing out is the buying process in reverse: you sell your crypto for your local currency on an exchange, then withdraw that balance to your bank. Knowing this before you start is reassuring — and it's part of using crypto sensibly.

Step 1 — Sell on the exchange

On the exchange where your crypto sits, sell the amount you want for your local currency. A market order sells right away at the current price; a limit order only sells at a price you set.

Step 2 — Withdraw to your bank

Withdraw the cash balance to a verified bank account in your name. Exchanges require identity verification (KYC) before withdrawals — that's normal and required by law.

Step 3 — Expect a fee and a short wait

A small withdrawal fee usually applies, and bank transfers can take anywhere from minutes to a few business days depending on your method and country. Check the fee and timing before you confirm.

Step 4 — Keep records for tax

In many places, selling crypto can be a taxable event. Keep a record of what you sold and when, and check your local rules — this page is educational only, not tax advice.
