What does HODL mean? (crypto slang, explained)

You'll see "HODL" everywhere in crypto. It started as a typo — and turned into a whole philosophy. Here's the story and the honest take.

The origin

A typo that became a movement

In an old forum post, someone meant to write "hold" and typed "HODL" instead. It stuck — now it's slang for holding through the ups and downs instead of panic-selling, often joked as "hold on for dear life".

What it really means

At its core, HODLing is a long-term mindset: ignore the daily noise, don't sell in fear, and think in years. It's the opposite of frantic trading on every price move.

The honest pros and cons

  • Pro: avoids panic-selling at the bottom and racks up fewer fees.
  • Pro: far less stressful than trying to time the market.
  • Con: it doesn't remove risk — the asset can still fall hard or never recover.
  • Con: "HODL no matter what" can mean holding a failing project too long.

Like any approach, it works best with real conviction in what you own — and only money you can afford to lose.
