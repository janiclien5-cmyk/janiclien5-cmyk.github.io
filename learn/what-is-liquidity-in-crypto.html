What is liquidity in crypto?

Liquidity decides whether you can actually get in and out of a coin at a fair price. It's a quiet but important factor — especially for smaller coins.

In one line

How easily you can buy or sell without moving the price

Liquidity is how easily an asset trades without shifting its price much. A highly liquid coin has lots of buyers and sellers, so trades fill quickly near the expected price. A low-liquidity coin can be hard to sell without crashing the price.

Why it matters

  • Spreads: low liquidity means a bigger gap between buy and sell prices.
  • Slippage: your trade can fill at a worse price (see slippage).
  • Getting stuck: you might not be able to sell at a fair price when you want to.

The beginner takeaway

Low liquidity is a big reason tiny, obscure coins are riskier than large, widely traded ones. Sticking to more liquid markets — at least while learning — keeps you out of the worst surprises.
