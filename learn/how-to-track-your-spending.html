How to track your spending (easy methods)

Tracking your spending is the single most eye-opening money habit — and it's simpler than it sounds. Pick a method you'll actually keep up.

Why it works

Awareness alone changes behavior

You don't have to budget hard yet — just see where the money goes. Most people are genuinely surprised by the small, frequent leaks, and seeing them is half the fix.

Pick a method (any of these)

  • An app: budgeting apps that import your transactions automatically.
  • A spreadsheet: simple columns for date, amount, category.
  • Pen and paper: jot every spend for a few weeks. Old-school but effective.

The best one is the one you'll actually stick with.

What to look for

  • The small, frequent spends that quietly add up.
  • Subscriptions you forgot you had.
  • Emotional or "autopilot" buys.
  • Your biggest categories — where small cuts make the biggest difference.

From here, a simple budget almost writes itself.

Curious about crypto?

Know your spare money first

Tracking your spending shows you exactly what's genuinely spare — and anything you'd put toward learning about crypto comes only from that, after your needs and savings. Build those basics first; then, if you're curious, learn about crypto carefully, only with money you can afford to lose. Start with the basics, safely.
