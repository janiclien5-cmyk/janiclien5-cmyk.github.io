Mining vs staking: what's the difference?

Both keep a blockchain secure and can earn rewards — but they work in very different ways. Here's the simple comparison.

The core difference

Computing power vs locked-up coins

Mining secures a network with computing power and energy. Staking secures it by locking up coins instead. Same goal — keeping the network honest — very different method.

How they compare

  • Energy: mining uses a lot; staking uses very little.
  • Hardware: mining needs specialized machines; staking just needs coins to lock.
  • Used by: mining secures Bitcoin; staking secures many newer "proof of stake" networks.
  • Rewards: both can pay rewards — and both are uncertain, not promised.

The honest risks

Neither is free money. Mining is costly and competitive; staking can lock your coins and they can still fall in value. With either, the underlying asset's price risk doesn't go away — only ever use money you can afford to lose.
