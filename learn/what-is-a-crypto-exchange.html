What is a crypto exchange? (a beginner's explainer)

It's where almost everyone's crypto journey starts. Here's what an exchange actually does and the two main types you'll hear about.

In one line

Where you buy, sell, and trade crypto

A crypto exchange is a platform where you swap regular money for crypto (and back), and trade one coin for another. For most beginners, it's the first place they get and learn about crypto.

Centralized vs decentralized

  • Centralized (CEX): run by a company that holds funds and matches trades. Easy for beginners — but you trust the platform, so security and reputation matter.
  • Decentralized (DEX): trade directly from your own wallet with no middleman. More advanced, with different risks like smart-contract bugs.

What to look for

Strong security, clear fees, an easy app, the coins you want, good support — and above all, that it's available in your country. See how to choose a crypto exchange and how to spot a fake one.
