What is leverage in crypto? (and why it's so risky)

Leverage promises bigger wins — and quietly delivers bigger, faster losses. Here's the honest beginner explanation, and why "start with leverage" is terrible advice.

Read this first

It multiplies losses, not just gains

Leverage means borrowing to control a position bigger than your own money. It cuts both ways: the same move that could double a gain can just as easily wipe out your deposit. It is not a beginner tool.

How it works (simply)

With, say, 10x leverage, a 1% price move becomes a 10% swing on your money. So a small move against you — just a few percent — can erase your entire deposit.

Liquidation: the wipeout

If the price moves against a leveraged position past a threshold, it gets liquidated — automatically closed at a loss, often taking most or all of your deposit. The higher the leverage, the smaller the move it takes to get there.

The honest beginner takeaway

Studies and broker disclosures consistently show most leveraged retail traders lose money, and leverage is one of the fastest ways beginners get wiped out. Learn the basics without leverage first. There is no rush, and there is no shortcut worth your whole deposit.
