Crypto vs stocks: what beginners should know

It's a common question, but the honest answer isn't "pick one." Here's how they actually differ — and what matters more than the choice.

The big difference

A stock is ownership; crypto often isn't

A stock is a share of a real company. A crypto token is a digital asset that may or may not represent ownership of anything — it depends entirely on the project. That difference drives most of the others.

How they compare

  • Hours: stocks trade on exchange hours; crypto trades 24/7.
  • Volatility: crypto is typically far more volatile, in both directions.
  • Regulation: stocks are heavily regulated; crypto far less so, and it varies by country.
  • Custody: with crypto you can hold your own keys — which means you carry the responsibility too.

Why it's not "either/or"

Plenty of people learn about both over time. The right mix depends on you — and this is education, not advice. What matters more than the label is the same for both.

What actually matters

Understand what you're buying, manage your risk, diversify sensibly, think long-term, and only use money you can afford to lose. Those habits travel across crypto, stocks, and everything else.
