What is a layer 2 in crypto? (explained simply)

Ever wonder why some crypto transactions are cheap and fast while others cost a fortune in gas? Layer 2s are a big part of the answer.

In one line

A faster, cheaper layer on top of a blockchain

A layer 2 is a network built on top of a main blockchain (the "layer 1") to make transactions faster and cheaper. It handles activity off the main chain and settles back to it — easing congestion and high fees.

A simple analogy

Think of the main chain as a busy main road that gets jammed (and pricey) at rush hour. A layer 2 is like an express lane built above it: most traffic flows there quickly and cheaply, then connects back to the main road. The base road still provides the underlying security.

The trade-offs

  • Pros: much lower fees and faster transactions.
  • Cons: added complexity, newer technology, and bridging funds between layers has its own risks.

For a beginner, the key takeaway is simply why fees and speeds differ so much between networks — and to be careful when moving funds across them.
