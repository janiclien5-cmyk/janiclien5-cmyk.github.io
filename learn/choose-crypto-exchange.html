How to choose a crypto exchange (a beginner's checklist)

Ignore the hype lists. For a beginner, a few practical things matter far more than which exchange is "trending" — here's what to actually check.

First filter

Is it available where you live?

Crypto services are restricted or unavailable in some regions, and features differ by country. Before anything else, check your local eligibility.

The checklist

  • Security. App-based 2FA, withdrawal protections, a solid track record.
  • Fees. Compare trading (maker/taker), deposit, and withdrawal costs — but don't pick on fees alone.
  • Ease of use. A clean app matters when you're learning; confusion causes mistakes.
  • Supported coins. Make sure it lists what you actually want to learn with.
  • Support & reputation. Responsive help and a history you can look up.
