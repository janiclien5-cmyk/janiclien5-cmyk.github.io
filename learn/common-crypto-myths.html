Common crypto myths (and the honest truth)

Crypto attracts strong opinions on both sides. Here are the myths beginners hear most — and the balanced, no-hype reality.

Myth 1

"It's all a scam"

Scams exist within crypto — but the technology itself is real and used worldwide. The skill is telling good projects from junk, not dismissing (or trusting) all of it.

Myth 2

"It's only for criminals"

Most crypto activity is legal, and public blockchains are actually traceable. Criminals use cash and banks too — the tool isn't the crime.

Myth 3

"A cheap coin is a bargain"

A low price per coin tells you nothing on its own. What matters is the total market cap and supply — a "cheap" coin can have billions of units and still be overvalued.

Myth 4

"You've already missed it"

Nobody can predict prices, so "too late" isn't really answerable — more on that here. Learning carefully beats chasing or despairing over a headline.

Myth 5

"You need a lot of money"

Most beginners start small on purpose, to learn. The goal early on is understanding, not size.
