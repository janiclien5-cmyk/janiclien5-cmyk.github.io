7 crypto mistakes beginners make (and how to avoid them)

Almost every beginner loss traces back to one of these. The good news: each has a simple fix you can apply from day one.

1

Buying on hype

Chasing a coin because it's "going up" or everyone's talking about it. Fix: learn what you're buying first; ignore FOMO.

2

Risking money they need

Putting in rent or emergency money. Fix: only use spare money, after your essentials and emergency fund are covered.

3

Skipping security

No 2FA, weak passwords, clicking any link. Fix: app-based 2FA, a unique password, and verify every link.

4

Sharing the seed phrase

Typing it into a "support" chat or a website. Fix: never share it with anyone, ever — no real team asks.

5

Falling for "easy profit" scams

Giveaways, doublers, sure-thing platforms. Fix: if it promises easy or sure-thing gains, walk away.

6

Going all in at once

Betting everything on one coin or one moment. Fix: start small, spread out, and never bet the farm.

7

Panic buying and selling

Buying the top in excitement, selling the bottom in fear. Fix: have a calm plan and zoom out from the daily noise.
