What is a memecoin? (an honest warning)

Memecoins are everywhere and easy to get sucked into. Here's what they actually are — and why caution matters more here than almost anywhere in crypto.

In one line

A coin based on a joke or trend, not a use case

A memecoin is built around a joke, animal, or internet moment rather than solving a problem. Its price runs almost entirely on hype and attention — which is exactly why it's so volatile.

Why they're so risky

  • Mostly hype: with little underneath, the price can collapse as fast as it rose.
  • Pump-and-dumps: insiders hype a coin, then sell on the crowd who bought in late.
  • Most go to ~zero: the large majority lose most or all of their value.
  • FOMO bait: "get in early" pressure is the classic way beginners get burned.

If you're still curious

Treat anything you put into a memecoin as money you're fully prepared to lose — because you very well might. Understand it's gambling-like, watch for scams, and never let hype rush your decision.
