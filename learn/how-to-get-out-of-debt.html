How to get out of debt (a simple plan)

Debt feels heavy, but a clear plan turns it from a vague dread into a series of small, doable steps. No shame, just a path.

Start here

List every debt in one place

Write down each balance, its interest rate, and the minimum payment. You can't beat what you can't see — and it's usually less scary on paper than in your head.

Pick a payoff method

  • Snowball: pay the smallest balance first for quick wins and momentum.
  • Avalanche: pay the highest interest rate first to save the most money.
  • Both work — choose the one you'll actually stick with.

Free up money to attack it

Use a simple budget to find extra room, always pay at least the minimums, and put every spare dollar at your target debt.

Stop the bleeding

Avoid taking on new high-interest debt while you climb out. Progress only sticks if the hole stops getting deeper.

Curious about crypto?

Clear high-interest debt first

Paying off high-interest debt is a near-certain win by removing that interest — usually better than any volatile bet. Clear it (and build a small emergency fund) before exploring crypto, and only ever with money you can afford to lose. Then learn the basics, safely.
