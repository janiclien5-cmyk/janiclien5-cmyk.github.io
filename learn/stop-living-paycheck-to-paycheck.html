How to stop living paycheck to paycheck

Breaking the cycle isn't about earning a fortune overnight — it's a few calm steps that give you breathing room. Here's where to start.

Step 1

See your numbers

Track your spending for a few weeks. You can't fix what you can't see — and the leaks and big costs are usually clearer (and less scary) on paper.

Step 2 — Build a small buffer

Even a tiny emergency fund breaks the cycle, because one surprise bill no longer sends you backwards. Start with a small target and grow it.

Step 3 — Automate saving (even a little)

Move a small amount to savings automatically on payday. Tiny and consistent beats big and never.

Step 4 — Shrink the big costs

Focus on your largest recurring expenses — housing, transport, subscriptions, and fees — rather than agonizing over tiny treats. Big levers move the needle.
