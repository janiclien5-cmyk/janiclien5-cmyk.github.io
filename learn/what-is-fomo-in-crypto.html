What is FOMO in crypto? (and how to beat it)

FOMO is the quiet force behind most beginner losses. Name it, spot it, and it loses most of its power over you.

In one line

The fear of missing out

FOMO is the urge to buy because a coin is shooting up and everyone seems to be getting rich. It feels urgent — and that urgency is exactly what makes it dangerous. It's a top reason beginners buy at the top, right before a drop.

The signs you're in it

  • You feel you have to act right now or you'll "miss it".
  • You're buying because of price and hype, not because you understand it.
  • Social media is screaming "to the moon" and you don't want to be left out.

How to beat it

  • Have a plan and stick to it, decided when you were calm.
  • Never act on a single hyped moment — sleep on it.
  • Remember: there's always another opportunity. Missing one isn't a loss.
  • Only use money you can afford to lose. A slow decision beats an urgent one.
