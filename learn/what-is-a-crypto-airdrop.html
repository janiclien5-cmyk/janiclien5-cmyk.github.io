What is a crypto airdrop? (and how to stay safe)

"Free crypto" sounds great — and that's exactly why fake airdrops are such a common trap. Here's what a real one is, and how to not get drained.

In one line

Free tokens sent to many wallets

An airdrop is when a project gives away tokens to lots of wallets — usually to reward early users or get attention. A genuine airdrop never asks you to pay or to share your seed phrase.

Why projects do it

  • To reward early users and build a community.
  • To spread awareness of a new token.
  • To decentralize who holds the token.

The scam version (very common)

Fake "airdrops" are everywhere. The usual trick: a message or site says you've won tokens, then asks you to connect your wallet and approve a transaction (which is actually draining it), or to enter your seed phrase to "claim." Both empty your wallet.

Stay safe

  • Never enter your seed phrase to claim anything.
  • Never pay a fee to receive "free" tokens.
  • Be wary of unexpected tokens that appear in your wallet — interacting with them can be the trap.
  • When unsure, do nothing. See how to avoid crypto scams.
